ZZSpace Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains how the ZZSpace development team handles your information. ZZSpace runs Android virtual machines (called spaces) on your Android device. You can use it without a ZZSpace account.

1. How we handle data

Your spaces, apps, settings, and files are stored locally. ZZSpace uses device, network, and runtime information to operate the app and diagnose problems. It does not automatically upload your space contents to the developer.

Diagnostics stay on your device unless you export and share them. Reports may include device details and logs; review them before sharing. If you contact us, we use your contact information, message, and attachments to respond and resolve issues.

We do not sell personal information or use your space contents for advertising.

2. Permissions and sharing

Features you enable may use:

  • Camera and microphone: Images and audio for apps inside a space, with Android permission, while the space is visible and your device is awake and unlocked.
  • Location and sensors: With Android location permission, apps in a visible space can use your approximate location. Compatible apps can use sensors while the space has focus.
  • Clipboard and files: Plain-text sharing while the space is visible, focused, and your device is unlocked; files or folders you select for import, export, sharing, or installation.

Control access through Android permissions and each space's settings. Apps inside a space may store or transmit data you make available to them.

3. GitHub and other services

Apps, websites, email, and file-sharing services you use handle information under their own privacy policies and may process it in other countries.

Our official project and issue tracker are hosted on GitHub. Visiting them is subject to the GitHub Privacy Statement. Public issue posts and attachments may be visible to others; send private requests by email.

4. Retention and deletion

Your spaces and settings remain until you delete them, clear ZZSpace's app storage, or uninstall the app. Exported files and copies held by other apps or services must be deleted separately. Third-party accounts follow the provider's deletion process.

We keep support messages and reports only as long as needed to handle the issue and meet legal obligations, then delete or de-identify them. You may request deletion of information under our control by email.

5. Security and policy changes

We use Android's private storage, permission controls, and controlled file sharing to protect local data. No system can guarantee complete security.

We may revise this policy and update the date above. We will provide additional notice and seek consent where required.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or requests to access, correct, or delete your personal information, email corespaceteam@gmail.com.
